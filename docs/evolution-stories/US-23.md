# US-23: Warm-start compatible searches and reuse evaluations

Issue [#41](https://github.com/ooples/AiDotNet.Evolution/issues/41). Implementation is entirely in
**AiDotNet.Evolution**. The runtime — bounded seed repertoires, twelve-facet revalidation, sample
provenance, persistent evaluation storage, and age/uncertainty/force-fresh decisions — is already on
the default branch. What this change adds is the part that was never carried across with it: the
paired campaign that exercises those contracts end to end, its retained raw evidence and offline
verifier, the analysis that checks the evidence, and a required CI job that runs all of it.

As a repeat user, I want compatible previous discoveries to accelerate new searches so that I do not
pay to rediscover or reevaluate the same work.

- [x] **Given** an exported repertoire, **when** a new run imports seeds, **then** genome schema,
  constraints, task semantics and provenance are validated; incompatible fitness is discarded and
  candidates are reevaluated. Revalidation and discard are exercised by the campaign's `expired`
  phase rather than asserted only in unit tests.
- [x] **Given** a persistent evaluation cache, **when** reuse is requested, **then** its key covers
  canonical code/genome, task/evaluator, data, fidelity, compiler/runtime, hardware and correctness
  policy as applicable. The campaign pins the source revision into every record and the verifier
  refuses an archive whose built assembly does not carry that revision.
- [x] **Given** stochastic or timing-based outcomes, **when** records are reused, **then** sample
  counts, uncertainty and freshness are preserved and new measurement remains possible; cache hits
  are not independent samples. The `force-fresh` phase pays for new measurement against a populated
  cache, and the analysis counts reused records separately from acquired samples.
- [x] **Given** a benchmark using warm starts, **when** systems are compared, **then** both receive
  equivalent admissible prior information and reports distinguish cold-start cost from the cost of
  building and reusing prior knowledge. Both arms receive byte-identical admissible inputs and prior
  cost is attributed in full.

## What this change ships

- `examples/PersistentEvaluation --campaign` — the paired no-reuse ablation, run as a mode of the
  existing example so it drives the real engine and the real persistent cache rather than a
  reimplementation of either. Two tasks, four phases, two reuse methods, primary and replay
  executions.
- `benchmarks/analysis/analyze_reuse.py` and its eight tests — raw-evidence, paired-prior,
  accounting and replay checks. One of those tests asserts that the CI gate requires the reuse job,
  so the gate cannot be quietly dropped.
- `benchmarks/evidence/noisy-reuse/3c0a680/` — the retained 32-seed campaign with an offline
  verifier that reads inside the archive without extracting or executing anything.
- `eng/Test-ReuseCampaign.ps1` and a required `noisy-reuse` CI job that runs the two-seed smoke
  against the commit's own source revision and uploads its evidence.

## Verification

Verified against this branch, not quoted from the archive:

- The offline verifier passes: source revision `3c0a6803d02bd0b6848a02a47137dec6db703ac4`, 1,152
  primary runs, 358,400 primary physical observations, 22,400 smoke observations, replay equal.
- The two-seed smoke campaign runs end to end and its analysis passes.
- All eight `test_reuse.py` cases pass.
- The `noisy-reuse` job is in `ci-gate`'s `needs` with its result checked, so the gate fails if the
  job does not succeed.

The retained campaign's own numbers are the archive's, measured at `3c0a680`; this change does not
re-run the 32-seed campaign. Warm reuse saves 40 observations per run in that fixed authored
ablation. That is a fixed effect under an authored protocol — not general quality superiority, not
net cold-start savings, and not a representative or physical timing-noise result. Hashes establish
consistency, not honest acquisition or statistical independence.
