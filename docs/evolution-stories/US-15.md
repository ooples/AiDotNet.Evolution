## User story and acceptance criteria

**Priority:** P1. **Owner:** Scheduler/evaluator maintainers. **Depends on:** US-05, US-06.

As a user training models or benchmarking many workloads, I want promising candidates to receive larger budgets so that full evaluation is concentrated where it helps.

- [x] **Given** increasing resource levels such as training epochs, dataset sizes, or benchmark repetitions, **when** a successive-halving/Hyperband-style policy runs, **then** it promotes selected candidates and resumes reusable evaluator state where valid. [Hyperband](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.06560).
- [x] **Given** one genome evaluated at several fidelities or repetitions, **when** outcomes are stored, **then** sample identity includes fidelity and replicate information; low-fidelity cache entries cannot satisfy full-fidelity requests.
- [x] **Given** noisy or poorly correlated early scores, **when** promotion decisions are audited, **then** an exploration allowance protects late improvers and only independently confirmed full-fidelity outcomes qualify for deployment.

The final criterion means full fresh confirmation is a necessary gate, never sufficient deployment permission. Exploration gives late improvers a chance, not guaranteed survival or universal improvement.

Deliverable: a resource-aware promotion scheduler and fidelity-specific sample records. This extends the existing fixed cascade rather than renaming it.

## Verified implementation

Production revision: `ac36140667911b47a955616b95ba00569041bc2a`.

- One bounded successive-halving bracket with greedy/exploratory promotion, fidelity/replicate sample identities, provenance-bound continuation tokens and fresh full confirmation. It is not a complete multi-bracket Hyperband implementation.
- `RunCheckpointedAsync` persists a checksummed settled prefix, latest owned tokens and exact ledger state. Resume validates run/seed/cohort/plan/evaluator/caps, sample identities, original receipt tombstones and promotion chronology before any new evaluator call. It recomputes control flow without recharging historical work.
- Actual gradient-descent training uses epochs 4/16/64, held-out prediction error, validated weight tokens, fixed data per search replicate, and separate full retraining/data for confirmation. Restart-only and full-cohort controls expose real epoch savings.
- 31 checkpoint tests cover eight boundaries, cancellation, failed/unknown measurements, zero diagnostic receipt retention, sink failure/mutation, changed inputs and rechecksummed corruption. A three-process test proves identical final model measurements, report and ledger, with no duplicate training epochs.

This is coordinated settled pause/restart, **not** arbitrary process-death recovery or durable in-flight worker leases (US-21). Checkpoint storage is trusted; checksum is not authentication. Checkpoint I/O, startup and rehydration CPU are not automatically priced.
