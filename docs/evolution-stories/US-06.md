## Scope and acceptance

Reuse the three core noise policies and their tests from `ab8af23` (historical
PR #49), then integrate independent timing confirmation into the current warm
comparison. Historical AiDotNet consumer results are not current delivery proof.

- **Given** a promising challenger, **when** `EvolutionIncumbentChallenge` runs,
  **then** use fresh challenger/incumbent search and confirmation batches, a
  predeclared finite challenge family, conservative bounded-mean intervals and
  persistent one-use slots. Unknown/incomplete work never confirms improvement.
- **Given** a frozen set of cheap-screen rejections, **when**
  `EvolutionRejectionAudit` runs, **then** select a predefined sample without
  replacement, charge fresh full evaluations and report a false-rejection interval
  that treats unresolved cases as potentially useful. Selection is invariant to
  input enumeration; the caller freezes the population and independent seed.
- **Given** fitness remeasurement, **when** repetitions or warmups run, **then**
  every invocation consumes budget. Warmups are not fitness observations;
  descriptor `Remeasure` does not substitute for new evaluator work.
- **Given** a selected warm program, **when** final confirmation runs, **then**
  interleave fresh original/selected timings in reproducible randomized order,
  use exactly the registered pair count and withhold all confirmation from search.
  Correctness alone cannot promote an unconfirmed timing improvement.
- **Given** an inconclusive, slower, invalid or insufficient-resolution result,
  **when** deployment is decided, **then** fall back to the independently audited
  original with deployed speedup 1. An invalid original aborts the comparison.
- **Given** changed decisions, missing/replayed samples, altered timing order or
  deployment metrics, **when** v4 evidence is reconciled, **then** reject it.
- **Given** either controller, **when** a v4 plan is admitted, **then** reserve all
  confirmation repetitions and eight final audit executions per track under the
  same US-05 budget rules before search starts.

## Statistical contract and limits

New execution requires `warm-head-to-head-v5`. Production registration fixes 16
paired timing observations and one-sided alpha `0.05 / (3 * planned_tracks)`;
three partitions each receive at most one third of the error budget. There is no
optional stopping, repeated attempt until success, or reuse of search timing.
The fixed small integration fixture uses three pairs to exercise plumbing, not
to estimate real comparative performance.

The promotion interval is Student's interval on paired log timing ratios. Its
nominal coverage assumes independent, approximately normal paired log ratios;
randomized interleaving mitigates order effects but does not prove independence.
Identical rounded ratios fail closed instead of implying infinite precision.
Runtime medians remain descriptive endpoints; this interval is specifically for
the geometric paired ratio, not a confidence interval for median latency.
Timing repetitions on the same task batch do not increase the independent search
sample count in the head-to-head analysis.

The reusable C# policies instead use predeclared bounded-quality intervals.
Caller-owned bounds, independence, correctness, data custody and replacement
remain obligations. Timing callbacks are trusted/cooperatively cancellable;
arbitrary program execution continues to use the existing Docker boundary.

Per-track confirmation capacity rises from `2 * 3 + 8 = 14` to
`2 * 16 + 8 = 40` container attempts. Search/model-call caps are unchanged.
No extra model calls, paid APIs, held-out studies or competitor wins are implied.
Previous v2/v3 reports remain historical; they cannot execute as v4 plans or
qualify as v4 predecessors.

The warm program comparison now has an opt-in cheap-fidelity screening stage,
enabled at registration with `--screening`. It uses one public search input at
full scale and one candidate execution (three for the original baseline), followed by the unchanged full evaluator for advancing
candidates. Correctness failures and clearly slow cheap observations reject early;
otherwise candidates advance. Only full-fidelity scores can enter selection.
After all searches in a task/seed block are frozen, a seeded sample of at most
two owner/candidate rejects across that block receives full-scale paired audits.
Their results never feed search.
See [screening integration and ablation](../SCREENING.md).

Worst-case capacity reserves screening for every evaluation, two additional
original-baseline observations per track and six full audit
executions per sampled reject, independently of search spending. This is deliberate
headroom, not a claim of actual savings. All-pass workloads can cost more with
screening, and small-input rankings can reverse at full scale. Screening stays
opt-in pending workload-specific cost/false-rejection acceptance evidence.
